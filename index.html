About us

Why CaloSwap feels different

CaloSwap is designed around a simple truth: most people do not want a completely different food, they want a smarter version of the one they already crave. That is why our platform focuses on recognisable favourites, clearer nutrition and paid alternatives that are easy to compare.

Instead of pushing bland “healthy eating” messages, we help you make a practical switch. You can start with a burger, fried chicken, pizza, sweet drink or loaded snack, see the calorie gap, and move to an alternative that still looks appealing, tastes familiar and fits everyday routines better.

Clear global positioning

Trust before purchase
